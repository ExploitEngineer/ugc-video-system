Feature preview - plain-language walk-through

Add a ready-made template to your finished video

Right now a run ends with your plain video clip. This feature adds one optional step: when the video is done, we ask if you'd like to drop it into a designed template - a branded frame, a lower-third caption, an intro/outro - and we render the finished, dressed-up version for you. This page shows exactly what that looks like, in plain English. Read it, then tell me your preferences in section 4.

Behind-the-scenes plumbing: built & tested The screens below: building next

1 How it works, in 3 steps

Nothing changes about how you make a video. This only adds a step at the very end.

1

Your video finishes, and we ask one question

A card appears in the chat: “Add a template?” with two buttons - Edit with a template or Skip. If you skip, you get your plain video exactly like today. Nothing forced.

2

If you say yes, you pick a design from a gallery

A page opens showing template designs, each with a little preview you can watch. Click the one you like.

3

We render your video into that template

Your clip drops into the design, we render it, and the finished templated video shows up in the chat - alongside your original, so you never lose it.

3 The two buttons, explained

These are the only two things you click on the question card.

→

Skip, keep it plain

You get your video exactly as it is today. No template, no extra render, no waiting. This is the same as how things work right now.

✨

Edit with a template

Opens the gallery. You pick a design, we render your clip into it, and you get a more polished, branded final video. Takes a little longer (a render step).

4 Your choices - tell me your preference

A few product decisions are genuinely up to you. I marked what I'd recommend, but click/annotate the option you want on each. If you're happy with all the recommended ones, just say “go with the recommended.”

A. When should the “Add a template?” question appear?
Every finished video, or only when you switch it on?
After every video Recommended

Simple and consistent - you're always offered the choice, and skipping is one click.

Only when I turn it on

The question is hidden by default; a setting enables it. Fewer prompts, but easy to forget it exists.

B. How should the gallery open?
A full page, or a pop-up over the chat?
A full page Recommended

Roomy grid, bigger previews - matches how the “Edit video” screen already works.

A pop-up over the chat

You stay on the run page. Quicker, but cramped for watching previews.

C. After templating, which video is “the” video?
The templated one is shown - what happens to the plain original?
Show the templated one, keep the original too Recommended

You can always fall back to the plain clip. Nothing is ever thrown away.

Replace it with the templated one

Cleaner, but the plain original is gone.

5 Good to know

Three things about how it behaves - no jargon.

🔒
Off until we switch it on

The feature is built but hidden. Your current runs finish exactly like today until we turn it on. No surprises.

🟩
Free to try first

There's a test mode that fakes the render (no cost, no account) so we can click through the whole thing before paying for the real service.

🎬
Real templates come later

The 3 designs shown are placeholders. Real ones need a designer to prep them - that's a separate later step.
