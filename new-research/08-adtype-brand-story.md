# 08 — Ad Type: Brand Story

> Goal: build emotional connection with the brand's mission/values through a short cinematic narrative. Feels like a mini-film, not a hard sell. Product is secondary to *meaning*.

---

## Theory — emotion + craft
- **Narrative over features.** A brand story earns attention with a small human arc (struggle → purpose → belonging). The product appears as the enabler, not the hero.
- **Cinematic craft signals quality.** Unlike UGC, here polish *is* the point — filmic lighting, deliberate camera, colour grade.
- **Consistency of world.** Same character(s), same colour palette, same tone across all 4 storyboards — this is where identity-lock + a fixed style anchor matter.
- **One emotional throughline.** Warmth, hope, resilience, pride — pick one and hold it.

## Narrative arc (60s → 4×15s)
1. Establish world + character + a relatable tension.
2. Turn — the moment of purpose / the brand's role appears.
3. Payoff — transformation / belonging / achievement.
4. Brand resolution — logo moment / tagline feel, emotionally satisfying.

---

## Image (gpt-image-2) guidance
- Style anchor: `cinematic film still`, consistent across frames.
- Lighting: motivated + filmic — `golden hour`, `soft window light with gentle shadow`, `warm practical light`. Can be a touch moodier than a service ad, but keep faces flattering.
- Lens: `35mm` (environmental storytelling) or `85mm` (emotional close-ups).
- Grade: name it once and reuse — `warm filmic grade, teal shadows / warm midtones`, or a film stock like `Portra 400`.
- Skin: soft-real line still applies; cinematic ≠ red/harsh.

**Template — cinematic story frame:**
```
Cinematic film still of a [character] in [meaningful setting], [emotional beat/action],
[one mood word] expression. Soft golden-hour light, gentle directional shadow,
warm filmic grade. 35mm lens, natural perspective, shallow depth of field.
Smooth healthy skin, balanced skin tone, soft editorial retouching.
Consistent with reference identity. No redness, no plastic skin, no text, no watermark.
```

## Video (Seedance 2.0) guidance
- Camera: deliberate, stabilised — `slow push-in` for emotion, `slow tracking` for journey, `subtle rise` for payoff. One move per clip.
- Motion: minimal, meaningful (a look, a turn, a hand reaching). Let the mood breathe.
- Audio: gentle music bed + ambient; sparse or no dialogue — let visuals carry it.
- Style: `cinematic film tone, 35mm, warm grade` (qualify cinematic, never alone).

**Template — image-to-video:**
```
Animate the provided image. Preserve identity, palette, and composition.
[Character] makes [one small meaningful movement]. Camera: slow push-in, stabilised.
Keep the warm golden-hour light and filmic grade. 15 seconds.
Avoid jitter, avoid identity drift, avoid temporal flicker.
```

---

## Prompt-enhancement rules (brand-story-specific)
- Lock a **single style anchor + grade** and repeat it across all 4 storyboard prompts (world consistency).
- Inject **motivated cinematic lighting** if missing.
- Keep **one camera move per clip**, all slow/stabilised.
- Preserve **one emotional throughline** — if the user's beats conflict emotionally, align them.
- Product stays subtle — don't let the enhancer over-feature it.

## Pitfalls / negatives
- Style drift between frames (breaks the "film"). → fixed anchor + identity lock.
- Over-busy motion (kills emotion). → one slow move.
- Moody-to-the-point-of-ugly faces. → keep skin soft/flattering.
- Turning it into a product ad. → keep meaning primary.
